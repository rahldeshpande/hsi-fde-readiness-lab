FDE Readiness Assessment

Assess your current operating capabilities, explore the gaps, and build a practical path toward Forward Deployed Engineering.

A developmental self-assessment, not a credential or employment classification. FTE measures staffing capacity; FDE describes a customer-facing engineering role. Scores reflect self-reported practice, with scenario checks providing additional evidence.

01 / Your context

Domain and system knowledge are context-specific. Capture your current environment and a target environment to evaluate what knowledge is portable.

Five capability pillars

Thinking: Critical, Systems, Strategic, Creative and Portable Thinking. Domain: business processes and context. Technical: engineering and systems knowledge. Human: communication and collaboration. Execution: ownership and outcomes.
