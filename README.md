HSI FDE Readiness Lab

Assess. Reflect. Evolve.

An interactive capability assessment and professional development lab for Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) and multidisciplinary operating models.

Part of HSI | Humans, Systems & Information, this lab explores how professionals can evolve beyond traditional role boundaries by combining thinking capabilities, domain expertise, technical proficiency, interpersonal skills, and execution ownership.

Why This Lab?

As technology, AI, and organizational operating models evolve, the boundaries between traditional professional roles are becoming increasingly fluid.

Forward Deployed Engineers represent an approach that combines engineering expertise, business understanding, customer engagement, and end-to-end problem ownership.

But readiness for such roles cannot be measured through technical proficiency alone.

This lab explores a broader question:

How prepared are professionals, managers, and leaders to operate effectively across disciplines, technologies, domains, and organizational environments?

The Five Capability Dimensions

The assessment evaluates five interconnected dimensions:

Dimension| What It Evaluates
Thinking Skills| Reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, and knowledge transfer
Domain Knowledge| Industry understanding, business processes, stakeholders, and business outcomes
Technical & Systems Knowledge| Engineering, architecture, integrations, platforms, and automation
Soft Skills| Communication, collaboration, adaptability, and influence
Execution & Ownership| Accountability, delivery effectiveness, governance, and measurable outcomes

Five Thinking Capabilities

The Thinking Skills dimension explores:

1. Critical Thinking: Challenge assumptions, evaluate evidence, and identify root causes.
2. Systems Thinking: Understand relationships, dependencies, and downstream consequences.
3. Strategic Thinking: Connect decisions to long-term objectives, priorities, and trade-offs.
4. Creative Thinking: Explore alternatives and develop solutions within constraints.
5. Portable Thinking: Transfer underlying principles across technologies, industries, and organizational contexts while recognizing important differences.

What's Inside?

1. Capability Assessment

Evaluate your capabilities across the five dimensions and identify areas of strength and development.

2. Scenario Challenge

Explore realistic professional situations that test decision-making beyond self-reported proficiency.

3. Retrospection & Portability

Reflect on past experiences, identify lessons learned, recognize transferable techniques, and understand what needs to change when moving between environments.

4. Insights & Development Roadmap

Visualize capability gaps and generate actionable 30/60/90-day development recommendations.

Who Is This For?

Individual Contributors: Professionals aspiring to develop multidisciplinary expertise, improve problem-solving, and take greater ownership of business outcomes.

Managers: Professionals responsible for developing versatile teams, managing delivery complexity, and balancing specialization with cross-functional capabilities.

Leaders: Professionals evaluating organizational capabilities, talent development, and emerging operating models.

The framework maintains common capability dimensions while adapting expectations to different levels of responsibility.

Understanding Your Results

The lab provides an indicative capability profile, not a professional certification.

Results are intended to support:

- Self-awareness and structured retrospection
- Identification of capability gaps
- Professional development planning
- Discussions about multidisciplinary readiness
- Exploration of emerging FDE operating models

Important: FDE and FTE are not mutually exclusive classifications. FDE describes a professional role and operating approach, whereas FTE (Full-Time Equivalent) is a staffing capacity measure.

This lab evaluates alignment with FDE-style capabilities rather than assigning individuals to exclusive employment categories.

Limitations

This is an exploratory assessment and development tool.

Self-assessments may be influenced by individual perception. Scenario responses provide additional perspective but do not independently validate workplace performance.

Results should not be used as the sole basis for hiring, promotions, or performance evaluations.

Domain-specific and technology-specific proficiency may require additional contextual assessment.
